Why Give

Invest in Community,
Memory & Legacy

At the St. Nicholas Terrace Neighborhood Foundation, we believe real change begins on the block — with neighbors, families, and shared history.

Our annual reunion on 129th Street and St. Nicholas Terrace is more than an event. It is a homecoming that honors our past and strengthens our future.

🍽
Food for the Community
Every family, every elder, every child gets fed. Your donation keeps the tables full and the love flowing.
🎶
Music & Entertainment
Live performances, DJs, and talent from Da Hill — keeping the culture alive on the block where it was born.
🎉
Youth Activities
Games, programs, and inspiration for the next generation of Da Hill leaders, scholars, and community builders.

Permits & Community Needs

Da Hill is not simply preserving memories — it is building the next generation through scholarships, health & wellness initiatives, entrepreneurship, arts, and community leadership rooted in the legacy of St. Nicholas Terrace, Harlem.

Your Sponsorship Supports

What You're Investing In

🏆
Youth Scholarships

Perseverance & Resilience, Leadership, Creative Arts, Entrepreneurship, and Community Service awards for Harlem youth ages 6–18.

❤
Health & Wellness Programs

The 45/45 Initiative brings free behavioral health evaluations and referrals to St. Nicholas Terrace and Convent Avenue residents.

📚
Educational Initiatives

Academic support, mentorship, and development programs that invest in the intellectual future of Da Hill's young people.

🎨
Arts & Creativity Programs

Spoken word, music, dance, photography, design — celebrating and nurturing the creative genius born on Da Hill.

🌟
Entrepreneurship Development

Business planning, mentorship, and funding pathways for young entrepreneurs from the community — connected to Black banking institutions.

🎉
Da Hill Reunion Celebration

The annual 3-day homecoming on St. Nicholas Terrace — where 40+ years of community, culture, music, food, and love come together.
