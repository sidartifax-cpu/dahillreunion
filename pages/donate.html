Why Give

Memory & Legacy

At the St. Nicholas Terrace Neighborhood Foundation, we believe real change begins on the block — with neighbors, families, and shared history.

Our annual reunion on 129th Street and St. Nicholas Terrace is more than an event. It is a homecoming that honors our past and strengthens our future.

🎉
