Common Questions

Frequently Asked

When is the next Da Hill Reunion?

Reunion dates are announced on our Facebook group first. Join the group at the link above to be the first to know when the next reunion is scheduled.

How do I get my business listed on the Entrepreneurs page?

Visit the Entrepreneurs page and fill out the submission form at the bottom. We review all submissions and publish within 5 business days.

How do I add a loved one to the Heroes tribute page?

Visit the Da Heroes page and use the tribute submission form at the bottom. You can also post in the Gone But Not Forgotten Facebook group.

How do I submit photos to the gallery?

Share photos directly on our Facebook group or email them to dahillreunion379@gmail.com with the subject line "Gallery Submission" and we will add them.

I grew up on Da Hill but moved away — can I still participate?

Absolutely. Da Hill is wherever you are. Distance doesn't change your roots. Join the Facebook group, come to the reunion, and connect with the family.

How can I support or sponsor Da Hill Reunion?

We welcome sponsors and community partners. Reach out through the contact form above with the subject "Partnership or Sponsorship" and we will connect with you.
