Who We Are

Honesty & Respect For
Our Hill Community

"Our Mission is to lovingly lead family and friends from Da Hill, back to Da Hill. To reunite, reconnect, and reclaim our love for this neighborhood."

To be a community that fosters the growth of our children, respects the diversity and humanity of all residents, and looks towards our elders for the resilience and strength to move forward in power.

There is a sound Da Hill makes — low and full of color, vibrant and bold. Made famous by our very own Kool Mo Dee. You can feel the energy and the love.
